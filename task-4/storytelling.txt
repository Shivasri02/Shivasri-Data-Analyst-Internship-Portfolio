## 📊 Data Story – Task 4

### 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data to identify key patterns, trends, and factors that influence revenue, enabling better business decision-making.

---

### 2. Dataset Description

The dataset consists of sales transactions including product categories, price, quantity sold, and total revenue generated. It provides insights into customer purchasing behavior and product performance.

---

### 3. Key Insights

* The Electronics category generates the highest revenue among all product categories.
* Sales are significantly higher on weekends compared to weekdays.
* There is an inverse relationship between price and quantity sold, indicating price sensitivity among customers.

---

### 4. Business Impact

These insights help businesses identify high-performing products, optimize pricing strategies, and focus marketing efforts on peak sales periods to maximize revenue.

---

### 5. Conclusion & Recommendation

Based on the analysis, it is recommended to prioritize high-performing categories like Electronics, implement targeted weekend promotions, and adopt competitive pricing strategies to boost overall sales performance.
